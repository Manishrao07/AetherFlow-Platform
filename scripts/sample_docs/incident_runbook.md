# Production Incident Runbook

Severity 1 incidents are customer-facing outages. Page the on-call engineer immediately and
open an incident ticket with severity SEV1. Post a status update every 30 minutes.

Severity 2 incidents are degraded performance without a full outage. Create a ticket with
severity SEV2 and notify the owning team channel. Updates are required every 2 hours.

After resolution, hold a blameless post-mortem within 5 business days. The post-mortem must
list the timeline, root cause, customer impact and follow-up actions with owners.

Escalation path: on-call engineer, then engineering manager, then head of engineering.
