# Refund and Returns Policy

Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of purchase if the product is unused.
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.

Refunds above 500 USD require manager approval before they are processed. Any refund that
exceeds 5000 USD must additionally be reviewed by the finance team.

Digital goods are refundable only if the licence key has not been activated.
Shipping fees are non-refundable unless the item arrived damaged or the wrong item was sent.
