# Information Security Policy

All employees must use multi-factor authentication for company systems. Passwords must be at
least 14 characters and must never be shared or written in tickets, chat or email.

Customer personal data may only be accessed for a documented business purpose. Exporting
customer data outside approved systems requires written approval from the security team.

Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the security team within 1 hour. Suspected
phishing emails should be forwarded to the security mailbox and not opened or answered.

Access reviews are performed every quarter. Accounts of departed employees are disabled on
their last working day.
